4. His DNA connected places we had been taught to imagine separately.

2. He lived about twenty-four thousand years ago, long before farming, cities, or written borders.
3. The site held carved ivory objects and ornaments, traces of skill and social life.
4. For decades, the burial was remarkable. Then scientists learned to read the DNA still held in ancient bone.

3. Think of it as finding shared spelling mistakes in copies of a very old book.
4. The more patterns two groups share, the more their histories may overlap, though the details still need testing.

**Visual reveal**
